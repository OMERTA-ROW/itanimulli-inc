# Itanimulli Inc. Community Hub

Build a website for ITANIMULLI INC., a community-focused nonprofit organization based in Conley, Georgia.

BRAND AND VISUAL IDENTITY — match this exactly, it comes from their existing letterhead:
- Background: deep near-black (#0A0A0A)
- Primary accent: soft mint green (#B8E6C1) — used for all text, borders, and the logo
- Every page/section framed by a thin double mint-green border inset from the edge, like a certificate or official document
- Logo: a mint-green filled circle with a large black serif letter "I" centered inside it, sitting on a black square
- Typography: wide letter-spacing on headings (like "I T A N I M U L L I   I N C ."), clean sans-serif body text in mint green, italic styling for body copy to echo the letterhead
- Overall feel: official, elegant, slightly mysterious, institutional

ORGANIZATION CONTENT:

Name: Itanimulli Inc.
Tagline idea: An evolving corporation, evolving alongside society.
Mission: Community membership, uplift, self-education, wealth and health productivity. The organization adopts, builds and uplifts communities. Every member is equal. Every community is different but faces the same equations in life, and Itanimulli Inc. works to eradicate those problems for better environmental enhancement.

Core philosophy to feature prominently: "Itanimulli Inc. is built to evolve and mold with time. Each member brings a new puzzle piece. As society reveals a new puzzle."

STRUCTURE — build this as three clear levels:

LEVEL ONE — The Foundation
The foundational basis of the organization:
- Organization Funding
- Grants
- School Grants
- Loans
- Society Association Gifts

LEVEL TWO — The Branches
The branches of Itanimulli Inc., described as "supreme pieces to the Itanimulli Inc. puzzle in society":
- New World Order Protesters Society
- Advocate Society
- Donations Society
- Community Outreach Society
- Vlog Society
Give each branch its own card with space for a description.

LEVEL THREE — Community Outreach
Recruitment combined with festivity and commemoration events. These functions are documented and posted under the Vlog Society.

DONATIONS SECTION:
"Itanimulli Inc. imbues our society into philanthropy." You do not have to be a member to join the Donations Society. Every donation, from $1 to millions of dollars, carries an invitation to organization involvement in projects and operations, in agreement with board members and society members. Each philanthropist or donor receives a certificate of donations for involvement in events and projects. Include a clear donate call-to-action (button can be a placeholder for now).

PAGES / SECTIONS NEEDED:
1. Home — hero with the logo, organization name, mission statement, and the "evolving corporation" philosophy
2. About — the full mission and the "each member brings a new puzzle piece" philosophy
3. The Three Levels — Level One, Level Two (branch cards), Level Three
4. Donations — the philanthropy section with donate CTA and certificate explanation
5. Membership / Join — a form to express interest in becoming a member
6. Contact — contact form, plus placeholder slots for address, phone and email (leave these as clearly-marked placeholders, do not invent real details)

Also include a placeholder section linking out to their blog site where members follow current and upcoming projects.

TECHNICAL:
- Fully responsive, mobile first
- Smooth subtle animations — nothing flashy, keep it dignified
- Accessible: the mint-on-black contrast must meet WCAG AA
- Clean semantic HTML, good SEO metadata

Leave all phone numbers, email addresses and external URLs as obvious placeholders — those details are still being finalized.
